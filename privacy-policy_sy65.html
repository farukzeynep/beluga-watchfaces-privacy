Privacy Policy

Beluga SY65 · Effective Date: November 7, 2026
Published by Beluga Watchfaces

Summary

Beluga SY65 does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

This policy covers the Beluga SY65 watch face for Wear OS (package com.belugawatchfaces.sy65) and its phone companion app, both published by Beluga Watchfaces.

  • The watch face contains no program code. It is a declarative Watch Face Format file that Wear OS draws on your watch.
  • It has no internet access, no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.
  • Everything it shows is read on the watch and stays on the watch.

Data Shown on the Watch Face

The watch face displays the following information. Wear OS provides it on the watch; the watch face only draws it and never saves or sends it anywhere.

Information Source Purpose
Time, date, battery level Wear OS system Shown on the dial and battery gauge
Step count Wear OS system Shown in the steps area; distance and calories are estimated from it on the watch
Heart rate Wear OS system Shown in the heart-rate area
Weather (condition, temperature, daily high/low) Your watch's weather service Shown in the weather area
Complication data Apps you choose in the watch face editor Shown in the customizable areas

Your customization choices (colors, language, complications) are saved by Wear OS on your watch, as part of the watch face settings.

Shortcuts on the watch face (Contacts, Music, Messages, Settings, battery status) only open the matching app on your watch; the watch face does not read any data from those apps.

Phone Companion App

The optional phone app only helps you install the watch face on your watch. It finds your paired Wear OS watch through Google Play services and opens the watch face's Play Store page on that watch.

It requests no permissions, collects no personal data and has no analytics or advertising.

Third-Party Services

Complication data comes from apps you choose yourself (for example Samsung Health or a weather app), and the Play Store and Google Play services are provided by Google. Those apps and services have their own privacy policies; Beluga Watchfaces receives no data from them.

Children

The watch face is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
