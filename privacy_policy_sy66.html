About

Beluga SY66 Watch Face is a Wear OS watch face built with Google's Watch Face Format. The watch face contains no executable code. An optional companion phone app only helps you open the watch face's Play Store page on your watch.

Data we collect

None. The watch face and the companion app do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device data. They contain no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.

Data shown on the watch face

Time, date, steps, heart rate, battery level, weather and complication data (for example calendar events or notifications) are provided by Wear OS and by the apps you choose. This data is only displayed on your watch and is never sent to us or to third parties. How that data is handled is governed by Wear OS and those apps.

Companion phone app

The phone app only checks which watches are connected to your phone (via Google Play services) so it can open the Play Store on the selected watch. This information stays on your devices and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The watch face is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
