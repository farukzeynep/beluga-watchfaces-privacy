Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BELUGA WATCHFACES ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our watch face applications ("Apps") available through Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

Our Apps are designed to function without collecting personal information. We do not independently collect, store, use, sell, or share personal information from users.

We do not collect:

  • Name or email address
  • Phone number or physical address
  • Location data
  • Photos, videos, contacts, or personal files
  • Financial or payment information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • User account information
  • Health or fitness information

2. Watch Face Data

Some watch faces may display information provided by the Wear OS operating system or user-selected complications, such as time, date, battery status, step count, or other information available on the user's smartwatch.

The watch face does not independently collect, store, transmit, or share this information with the developer or with our servers. Information provided by the watch or operating system is processed according to the platform's functionality and permissions.

3. Data Storage

Our Apps do not maintain a remote database containing personal user information. Any preferences or settings required for the operation of a watch face are stored locally on the user's device or smartwatch, where applicable.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Because our Apps do not collect personal information, we do not have personal information collected by the Apps to sell or share.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may independently collect information in accordance with Google's own policies and terms. Such data collection is controlled by Google and is not controlled by us.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Payments and Purchases

If you purchase a paid watch face or make an in-app purchase, payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your credit card, debit card, bank account, or other payment information.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

Because our Apps do not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a database of user personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
